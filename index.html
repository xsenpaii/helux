Shipping Policy

Free delivery on orders above ₹999 across India.

Your order is placed with our supply partner after we confirm your payment, usually within 1–3 business days. Total delivery time is usually 5–10 days across India, and can be longer in remote areas.

Once your order ships, the courier tracking link appears under Track Your Order. We do not ship outside India yet.

Refund & Returns

If your item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Share your Order ID, clear photos, and an unboxing video if you have one.

After we verify the issue, we will send a replacement or refund your payment. Refunds go back to your UPI account within 5–7 working days.

Because these are skincare products, we cannot accept returns for opened items or change-of-mind returns. We do not accept returns of used products.

Privacy Policy

We collect your name, phone number, email and delivery address only to process, deliver and support your order.

To fulfil your order, we share your name, phone number and delivery address with our supply and courier partners. We do not sell your data to anyone else.

Account sign-in and order records are stored with our database provider (Supabase). We never see your UPI PIN or bank details. To ask for your data to be deleted, use the Contact Support page.

Terms of Service

Prices are in Indian rupees and include applicable taxes.

An order is confirmed after we match your payment to our records. If we cannot find your payment within 24 hours, we will contact you.

We may cancel orders affected by pricing or stock errors and will refund any payment received. Skin results vary. Do a patch test before first use. These terms follow the laws of India.
